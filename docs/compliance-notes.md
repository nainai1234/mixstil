# Compliance Notes

## Copyright

Every sound asset must record:

- Source.
- License.
- Commercial usage permission.
- Derivative-work permission.
- Platform redistribution permission.
- Attribution requirement.
- Expiration or renewal terms.

Public-domain composition does not automatically mean a modern recording is public domain. Recordings, samples, loops, voice clips, and generated audio should be tracked separately.

## UGC Safety

Before opening public creation and sharing at scale, the product needs:

- Content filtering for titles, descriptions, tags, and covers.
- Report flow.
- Block or hide creator flow.
- Platform contact channel.
- Manual moderation queue.
- Takedown flow.
- Repeat-violator policy.

## Medical And Wellness Claims

The product should avoid medical claims.

Forbidden positioning:

- Treat insomnia.
- Cure anxiety.
- Repair trauma.
- Replace medical care.
- Guarantee sleep.

Safer positioning:

- Sleep and relaxation soundscape.
- Mindful audio.
- Bedtime listening.
- Calm atmosphere.
- Breathing practice companion.

## App Store Payment Boundary

Tool subscription is the safest first monetization layer:

- Premium assets.
- Longer generation.
- More storage.
- Advanced analytics.
- Export options.

When creator content becomes purchasable inside iOS, payment flows must be reviewed against Apple In-App Purchase rules. Real creator payouts and revenue share should wait until moderation, copyright, tax, and payout operations are ready.
