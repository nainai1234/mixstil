## Claim corrections

- High public views establish discovery and repeated consumption, not that a
  structure improves sleep, meditation, coding, or concentration.
- The global usage of `Koto`, `Flower Dance`, `Kerala`, or `Aruarian Dance`
  does not prove causal Focus effectiveness. It supports demand for
  instrumental electronic/Lo-fi styles.
- “Pure instrumental”, “no percussion”, “low attack”, and “no voice” must be
  verified from the exact recording, not inferred from artist, album, title,
  genre, or another live/studio version.
- Cultural instrument identity must be attached to an exact source and real
  instrument. It cannot be generated by relabelling a generic synth patch.
