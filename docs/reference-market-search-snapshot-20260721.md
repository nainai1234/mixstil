## Interpretation boundary

- Search results are biased by channel size, SEO, autoplay, thumbnail, age,
  and platform distribution.
- High views do not mean a track is suitable for Sleep or Focus; several
  results may contain jazz, beats, vocal-like pads, or large dynamic arcs.
- Each candidate must still pass a listening review and a structural analysis.
- No audio is downloaded, copied, or used as model input by this snapshot.
