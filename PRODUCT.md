# Product

## Register

product

## Positioning

MixStil is a ToC-first personalized soundscape product for consumers who cannot
reliably find a fixed sleep, calm, or focus sound that matches their specific
preferences and exclusions.

## Product Purpose

A consumer describes the sound they need and receives, within seconds, a
fitting and immediately playable personalized soundscape. The user can refine
it through continued AI conversation, make precise adjustments when needed,
save a reproducible version to My Sounds, and return to it later without
starting over.

The paid value is persistent personalization: preference memory, saved versions
and variants, longer and offline listening, cross-device replay, and sufficient
generation and refinement capacity.

## Primary User

The initial user already listens to sleep sounds, noise, ambient music, or
focus audio, but finds existing tracks unsuitable because of unwanted sound
families, sudden events, obvious loops, excessive music or voice, or the lack
of local adjustment.

Small businesses and creators may use the same advanced controls later, but
they are not the current acquisition, information architecture, or development
priority.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, personal, and trustworthy. The interface lowers cognitive load,
states capability honestly, and feels like a reliable listening service rather
than a medical product, social platform, or experimental audio playground.

## Design Principles

1. Result first: one sentence leads to a playable result before advanced tools.
2. Conversation first: AI refinement is primary; manual controls provide
   precision when requested.
3. My Sounds creates retention through saved, trusted, reproducible versions.
4. Explicit preferences and exclusions always outrank inference.
5. Live Mix, saved versions, offline playback, and rendering share Recipe V2.
6. The subscription sells persistent personalization and reliable replay, not
   internal audio-engine complexity.
7. Voice-free Beta makes no production voice claim.
8. No medical, therapeutic, frequency-effect, or guaranteed-outcome claims.

## Accessibility And Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, focus, labels, and interaction semantics.
Support keyboard operation, screen-reader status, reduced motion, system
language defaults, lock-screen playback, and understandable state without
depending on color or animation alone.
