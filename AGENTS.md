# Project Execution Guardrails

Before starting every task, tell the user both of the following in a short commentary update:

1. Current mainline task: execute Sprint 1 of the ToC loop: reliable mobile background playback, interruption and resume handling, long-session stability, system media controls, and playback-state recovery.
2. Final product goal: a consumer describes the sound they need and receives, within seconds, a fitting and immediately playable personalized soundscape that can be refined, saved, reliably replayed, and improved over time from explicit preferences.

Read `docs/project-mainline-charter.md`, `docs/toc-product-development-master-plan.md`, and `docs/personalized-audio-generation-plan-v2.md` before selecting implementation work. The ToC master plan overrides creator-first PRDs and backlogs when they conflict. Subscription, trials, entitlements, and payment are the final Sprint and must not be implemented before the consumer value, replay, offline, and mobile distribution loops are complete. Prefer the current Sprint exit criteria over peripheral polish. Do not prioritize small-business workspaces, creator marketplaces, export edge cases, generic AI music generation, large undirected asset expansion, social features, or external model experiments unless the mainline plan explicitly reaches them.
